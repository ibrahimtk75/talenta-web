Savoir-Faire

Made slowly, on purpose.

I. The Crown

Only the heart of the hide

Full-quill ostrich is defined by its raised follicles, which gather most densely across the crown. We cut from this area alone, so every piece wears the pattern at its most dramatic.

II. The Stitch

Saddle-stitched by hand

Two needles, one waxed linen thread, each stitch locked independently. Should one ever break, the seam holds — the reason saddlers have worked this way for centuries.

III. The Edge

Painted, sanded, repeated

Edges are hand-painted and burnished in successive layers until smooth as lacquer, then lined in full-grain calfskin that softens beautifully with use.
