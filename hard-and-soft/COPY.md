## Flagship — Wallet 6cc in Pure Full-Quill Ostrich Leather

**Subtitle:** The signature bifold, cut from the crown of South African full-quill ostrich.
**Price:** AED 2,450 / $680 · **Ref.** HS-OS-W6
**Colours:** Obsidian Black · Cognac Tan · Espresso Ebony · Midnight Navy · Sahara Khaki

**Description**

There are objects you carry, and objects that carry something of you. The Wallet 6cc belongs to the second kind.

Each piece is cut from the crown of a single South African full-quill ostrich hide. That is the most coveted area of the skin, where the raised follicles gather into their densest, most dramatic pattern. No two crowns are alike, so no two wallets are alike: yours is, quite literally, one of one.

Inside, a buttery full-grain calfskin lining frames six credit card slots, two full-length banknote compartments and two discreet slip pockets for receipts and cards you reach for less often. Edges are hand-painted and burnished in successive layers, and every seam is saddle-stitched by hand. The technique is slower than any machine, and far more enduring.

A quiet, hand-centred metallic emblem rests in the lower corner: a signature, never a shout.

**Dimensions & Specifications**
- Dimensions: 115 mm × 8 mm × 90 mm (W × D × H)
- Weight: 85 g
- 6 credit card slots · 2 banknote compartments · 2 slip pockets
- Exterior: 100% full-quill ostrich leather, South Africa
- Interior: smooth full-grain calfskin lining
- Hardware: hand-centred metallic emblem

**Craftsmanship & Care**
- *The leather:* Full-quill ostrich is prized for its raised follicles, natural oils and exceptional resistance to cracking. Differences in quill density and tone are the hallmark of a genuine hide, never a flaw.
- *The making:* Cut by hand, saddle-stitched with waxed linen thread and edge-painted in successive layers. Every piece passes a final hand inspection before it is signed with our emblem.
- *Care:* Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth. Condition two to three times a year with a neutral exotic-leather cream, working along the quills. Keep away from prolonged sunlight, heat and moisture, and store in the dust bag provided.
